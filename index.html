Santeo Residences

A contemporary five-storey residential development in Santeo, Greater Accra. The first to third floors accommodate apartment units, while the fourth and fifth floors form an exclusive three-bedroom penthouse.

The penthouse extends the living experience outdoors with a sky pool and bar area, together with dedicated seating and a barbecue lounge.

Building Five-Storey Development
Floors 1–3 Apartment Units
Floors 4–5 3-Bedroom Penthouse
Signature Amenities Sky Pool · Bar · Lounge · Barbecue

A luxury bathroom renovation designed around a calm, spa-inspired atmosphere. The proposed scheme brings together warm timber finishes, natural stone, a bespoke vanity, freestanding bathtub, black fittings and integrated lighting.

The project documents the journey from the existing bathroom through construction progress to the proposed design vision.
